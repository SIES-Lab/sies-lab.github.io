# Sources and verification notes

This website is an original SIES Laboratory implementation. The reference website is used only as inspiration for the broad information architecture and editorial flow; its logo, writing, photographs, testimonials, code and research content are not reused.

## Reference for structure and layout

- Sustainability Lab, IIT Gandhinagar: https://sustainability-lab.github.io/
- Join the lab page: https://sustainability-lab.github.io/join.html
- Publications page: https://sustainability-lab.github.io/papers/index.html

The SIES homepage adapts the reference site's high-level progression: introductory statement and join links; research themes; join & learn; life in the lab; selected work; latest updates; useful resources; footer.

The site links selected publications by DOI. The curated list should be checked against the PI's current Google Scholar profile or CV before public release. Google Scholar URL has not yet been supplied directly for this build.

## Content intentionally not invented

No lab photographs, student testimonials, group-member biographies, awards, sponsorships, active vacancies or lab-specific social media accounts were fabricated. The Life in the Lab area is an intentional placeholder for approved photos and stories. Join information is enquiry guidance and does not imply current open funded positions.

## Visual identity

- The complete SIES laboratory logo lock-up was supplied by the laboratory and is used in full; it is not replaced with a cropped emblem or a separate text approximation.
- The homepage energy-system illustration was newly created for this website. Its HTML legend describes the conceptual elements shown; it is an illustrative overview rather than a measured single-line electrical diagram.
