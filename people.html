Dr. Payal Vyankat Dahiwale

Dr. Payal Vyankat Dahiwale received her M.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar in 2019 and her Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay in 2026. She worked at Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) Research and Innovation Labs during August–December 2019.

Her research focuses on modern power systems, with emphasis on electric vehicle integration, smart charging, renewable energy integration, battery energy storage systems, power-system optimisation and intelligent energy management. Her work combines mathematical modelling, optimisation and control techniques to develop scalable solutions for coordinated operation of EVs and distributed energy resources in future smart grids.
