Research directions

Intelligence for tomorrow’s
energy networks.

We combine power-system insight with mathematical modelling, optimisation and intelligent control to develop solutions for sustainable, reliable and flexible energy systems.

01 · Mobility meets the grid

Electric vehicle integration & smart charging

Large-scale EV adoption introduces new flexibility—and new operational challenges—for power distribution networks. Our work explores coordinated and intelligent charging strategies that account for the needs of EV users, aggregators and network operators.

We investigate charging coordination across centralised, decentralised, hierarchical and multi-agent settings, with attention to feeder and voltage constraints, peak demand, charging cost and user requirements.

Smart chargingEV aggregatorsTransactive energyDistribution networksV2X
02 · Flexible, low-carbon systems

Renewable energy & battery storage integration

Increasing shares of variable renewable generation call for coordinated operation of generation, storage and flexible demand. We are interested in modelling and optimisation approaches that help integrate distributed energy resources into modern power systems.

Research directions include battery energy storage, renewable-aware scheduling, distributed energy resource coordination and the techno-economic aspects of sustainable electrification.

Renewable integrationBattery storageDistributed resourcesTechno-economics
03 · Models that support decisions

Intelligent energy management & optimisation

Future energy systems are increasingly interconnected and data-rich. We use mathematical modelling, optimisation and control to develop decision-support methods for coordinated system operation.

Our interests include multi-objective optimisation, intelligent energy management, demand response, multi-agent coordination, distributed scheduling and the development of computational tools.

Mathematical modellingOptimisationControlDemand responseTools development
04 · Evidence for transition

Energy policy & sustainable mobility

Technology transitions are shaped by policy choices and implementation realities. We study quantitative methods to assess EV and energy-policy interventions and identify effective pathways for accelerating sustainable mobility.

Our work connects policy objectives with measurable indicators, optimisation-based intervention selection and techno-economic analysis.

EV policy analysisPolicy implementationSustainable mobilityQuantitative assessment
How we work

Methods & research toolkit

Across these themes, our approach combines first-principles understanding of power systems with rigorous computational methods. We develop mathematical formulations, build optimisation models, evaluate control strategies and create tools that support reproducible analysis.

Model & formulate

Translate practical energy-system challenges into mathematical models, constraints and objective functions.

Optimise & coordinate

Design algorithms and coordination mechanisms for multiple assets, users and system stakeholders.

Evaluate & validate

Compare methods through simulation, benchmark systems and relevant operating scenarios.

Translate to impact

Connect technical findings to grid operation, user needs and policy decisions.
