Research opportunities depend on institute admissions, project availability and supervision capacity. Please use this page to introduce your interests and explore whether they align with the laboratory's work.

01 / Prospective students

PhD & research students

Students interested in EV integration, smart charging, renewable energy, battery storage, power-system optimisation or intelligent energy management are welcome to write and discuss research fit.

  • Briefly describe your academic background
  • Identify the research theme that interests you
  • Share relevant coursework, projects or publications
Enquire about research →
02 / Student projects

Internships & project enquiries

Undergraduate and postgraduate students may contact the laboratory to ask about possible short-term or semester research projects. Opportunities depend on the research programme and availability.

  • State your preferred dates and duration
  • Summarise technical skills and prior work
  • Attach a current CV or resume
Discuss a project →
03 / Research partnerships

Academic & industry collaboration

We welcome interdisciplinary conversations with academic, government and industry partners on power systems, transport electrification and sustainable energy.

  • Outline the challenge or research question
  • Share relevant technical or institutional context
  • Suggest a possible collaboration pathway
Start a collaboration →
