Doctoral positions03 OPENINGS

PhD students

Full-time and part-time options available

We invite applications from motivated candidates interested in one or more of these research areas:

  • Electric vehicle integration, smart charging and vehicle-to-grid (V2G)
  • Renewable energy integration and microgrids
  • Battery energy storage and distributed energy resources
  • Power distribution networks, grid optimisation and demand response
  • Intelligent energy management, modelling and data-driven operation
  • Other interdisciplinary areas and not limitted to above mentioned areas
